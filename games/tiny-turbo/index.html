How to Play

Hold ◀ ▶ to steer, ▲ for gas, ▼ to brake and N₂O for nitro. Tap Back on track if you get stuck.

Finish 3 laps first. The grass slows you down, so stay on the asphalt. Gold coins respawn every lap, and blue canisters refill nitro. Win coins to buy paint, bodies, spoilers, wheels, decals and performance upgrades.

Race Friends

Up to 4 players race on the same track. Friends show up as see-through ghost cars that can't hit you.
