How to Play

Every course has a hazard: mud in Pine Valley, quicksand in Sunset Dunes, ice in Frost Peak and shock pads in Neon City. They're hard to spot, so watch the road. Sunset Dunes, Frost Peak and Neon City have cliff jumps that are too wide to clear without nitro. Fall in and you wait 3 seconds. Blue canisters before each jump refill your nitro. Use nitro in the first 3 seconds and you're frozen for 3.

Hold ◀ ▶ to steer and ▲ to drive. Hold NITRO to drive with a boost. ▼ brakes. Tap ↺ if you get stuck, or ⏸ to pause.

Finish 3 laps first. The grass slows you down, so stay on the asphalt. Gold coins respawn every lap, and blue canisters refill nitro. Win coins to buy paint, bodies, spoilers, wheels, decals and performance upgrades.

Race Friends

Up to 6 players race on the same track, at the host's speed class. Friends show up as see-through ghost cars that can't hit you.
